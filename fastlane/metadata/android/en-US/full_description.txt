Apex Music Player is a powerful, lightweight, and privacy-focused open-source music player engineered for ALL Android smartphones and tablets (Android 5.0 to Android 15+).

UNIVERSAL COMPATIBILITY:
Runs seamlessly on any device: Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO, Huawei, OnePlus, Motorola, Sony, Realme, and others.

FEATURES:
• Universal Android Support: Full support from Android 5.0 Lollipop through Android 15 (API 21 to 35). Features native Edge-to-Edge display on Android 15 and deep OLED True Black theme.
• YouTube Downloader: Search YouTube directly inside the app and download high-quality audio streams along with embedded album cover artwork.
• Custom Playlists System: Create unlimited custom playlists with customizable emoji badges. Add tracks, reorder, sort by artist or title, and manage favorites.
• Uninterrupted Background Playback: Built on an Android Foreground Service with lock-screen controls, media notification shade actions (Play, Pause, Next, Prev), and headset button support (single, double, and triple click).
• State Preservation: Never lose your playback queue or current track timestamp when Android clears memory. Playback state is automatically saved and restored seamlessly.
• Convenience Controls: Playback speed control (0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x), ±10-second fast seek jump buttons, instant search filter, and custom sorting.
• Equalizer & Visualizer: 10-band equalizer presets with real-time 3D audio-reactive spectrum visualizations.
• 100% Free & Open Source: No advertisements, no trackers, no Google Play Services dependency. Licensed under GNU GPL v3.
